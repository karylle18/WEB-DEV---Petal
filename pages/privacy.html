Our privacy promises

  • No cookies.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No outside libraries, fonts or images.
  • No network requests. Petal never sends your data anywhere.
  • No email or phone number needed to sign up.

What Petal stores

  • Account (localStorage): display name, username, bio, and a password hash.
  • Posts (localStorage): text, likes, and when the post fades.
  • Session (sessionStorage): your username, until you close the tab.

You can view, download or delete all of it on the Settings page.

Honest limits

Petal is a prototype. Because it has no server, anyone who can use this browser profile could open the developer tools and see the stored data. A real platform would need a secure backend with end-to-end encryption.
