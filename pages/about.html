About Petal

AI systems are getting better at reading everything we write. Petal is a small prototype that asks: what if a social platform simply did not collect anything?

How it works

  • Built with only HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No backend, database or API.
  • Accounts and posts are saved in your browser's localStorage.
  • Your login session lives in sessionStorage, so it ends when you close the tab.
  • Passwords are never saved as typed. They are turned into a SHA-256 hash first.
